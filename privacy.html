Privacy Policy – SchichtSync

Last updated: October 2026

In short: SchichtSync does not collect any data from you. There is no provider server, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your shifts, plans and photos stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Controller

What data does the app process?

Only what you enter yourself: name, shifts, shift types, vacation, plans, work places, optionally a home address and profile pictures.

  • Storage: Data lives on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, Apple syncs it between your devices via iCloud (CloudKit) in your private iCloud area. The app provider has no access and receives no copy.
  • Planning together: If you invite someone, Apple shares the plan through an iCloud share. Both people see only that plan. You can stop sharing at any time (“More → Manage sharing”).
  • Calendar: If you turn it on, the app writes your shifts into its own calendar “SchichtSync”. iOS asks for permission. The app does not read other events.
  • Notifications: Reminders and change alerts are created locally on your device. iOS asks for permission.
  • Camera and photos: Only when you take or pick a profile or background picture. It stays in your app and your iCloud.
  • Distance to work: If you enter a home address and work places, Apple Maps calculates the distance. The addresses are sent to Apple (see Apple’s privacy information for Maps). The app provider does not receive them.
  • Public holidays: Built into the app, nothing is downloaded.

Sharing, tracking, ads

No data is shared with third parties. There is no tracking, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party libraries.

Backup and deletion

“More → Backup” exports everything to a file. Deleting the app removes the data from the device. You can delete the iCloud copy in iPhone Settings under “Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → SchichtSync”.

Your rights

Because the provider receives no data from you, it can neither view nor change it. For GDPR rights regarding data held by Apple, please refer to Apple. Questions about this policy: schubert.alexander@me.com
